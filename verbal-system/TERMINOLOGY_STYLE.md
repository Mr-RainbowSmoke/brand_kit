# Terminology And Style Conventions

## Naming And Capitalization
- Primary brand name: RAINBOWSMOKE
- Person/brand operator: Mr. RainbowSmoke
- Legal entity reference: RAINBOWSMOKE LLC
- Approved short icon reference: RS

## Preferred Framing
- Preferred: creator network, community, live entertainment, creative content
- Avoid defaulting to: platform-first, agency-first, or purely transactional language

## Tone Safety
- Keep language inclusive, welcoming, and specific.
- Avoid generic diversity claims without community context.
- Avoid over-corporate phrasing that suppresses brand personality.

## Grammar Conventions (Baseline)
- Sentence case for body copy and UI labels unless campaign art direction requires all caps.
- Keep sentences concise and direct.
- Prefer active voice.
- Avoid stacked buzzwords.

## Banned Or Discouraged Patterns
- Empty hype with no clear value statement.
- Exclusionary language or identity assumptions.
- Visual-first statements that ignore accessibility and readability.
