# Voice And Tone

## Voice Principles
- Bold
- Inclusive
- Community-forward
- Energetic but human

## We Are / We Are Not
| We Are | We Are Not |
|---|---|
| Bold and expressive | Flat or generic |
| Inclusive and authentic | Sanitized or tokenized |
| Blue-forward Pride identity | Muted, ambiguous identity |
| Community-first | Transaction-first |
| Warm and direct | Stiff corporate language |

## Voice Is Constant
The brand voice should remain recognizable across channels.

## Tone Is Contextual
Tone adapts by context while preserving core voice.

## Tone Matrix
| Context | Formality | Energy | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social | Low | High | Conversational and participatory. Keep hooks short. |
| Website | Medium | Medium-high | Warm and clear with strong hierarchy. |
| Campaign | Medium | High | Motivating language with clear action cues. |
| Support | Medium | Low-medium | Calm, specific, empathetic, and solution-first. |
| Media and partner | Medium-high | Medium | Professional and concise, with brand-safe terminology. |

## Voice Attributes
### Bold and maximalist
- Use language that signals confidence and motion.
- Avoid timid copy and over-qualified statements.

### Inclusive and authentic
- Keep language welcoming across identities and communities.
- Avoid generic diversity filler and identity assumptions.

### Community-first
- Prioritize people, creators, and shared participation in framing.
- Avoid platform-centric copy that erases community context.

### Energetic and warm
- Prefer direct active language.
- Keep momentum without sacrificing clarity.

## Writing Do
- Use direct language with personality.
- Keep messaging inclusive and welcoming.
- Lead with community and creator value.

## Writing Don't
- Avoid sterile or overly corporate copy.
- Avoid vague hype with no clear message.
- Avoid language that excludes identity or community context.

## Channel Notes
- Social: short hooks, strong momentum, clear CTA.
- Website: clarity and navigation readability first.
- Support: empathy and resolution clarity first.
- Media and partner: concise delivery with canonical naming.

## Terminology Baseline
- Use RAINBOWSMOKE for primary brand reference.
- Use RS for approved monogram/icon context.
