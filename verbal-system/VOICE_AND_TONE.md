# Voice And Tone

## Voice Principles
- Bold
- Inclusive
- Community-forward
- Energetic but human

## Voice Is Constant
The brand voice should remain recognizable across channels.

## Tone Is Contextual
Tone adapts by context while preserving core voice.

## Tone Matrix (Initial Canonical Draft)
| Context | Tone | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Social | High-energy, conversational | Invite participation and expression. |
| Website | Warm, confident, clear | Keep mission and navigation easy to understand. |
| Campaign | Bold, motivating, expressive | Use stronger calls to action and momentum language. |
| Support | Calm, helpful, empathetic | Prioritize clarity and care over hype. |
| Media and partner | Professional, concise, brand-safe | Keep messaging direct and consistent with approved terminology. |

## Writing Do
- Use direct language with personality.
- Keep messaging inclusive and welcoming.
- Lead with community and creator value.

## Writing Don't
- Avoid sterile or overly corporate copy.
- Avoid vague hype with no clear message.
- Avoid language that excludes identity or community context.

## Terminology Baseline
- Use RAINBOWSMOKE for primary brand reference.
- Use RS for approved monogram/icon context.
