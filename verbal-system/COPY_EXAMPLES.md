## Support
### Helpful responses
- Thanks for reaching out. We can help you get this sorted quickly.
- We hear you. Here is the fastest path to fix this.

- We could not process that request yet. Try once more, or contact us directly.

- Keep messaging direct, inclusive, and specific.
- Match intensity to context; do not force high-energy language in support contexts.
