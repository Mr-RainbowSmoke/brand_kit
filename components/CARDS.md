# Cards

## Purpose
Define content card layout for web, social previews, and community highlights.

## Card Anatomy
- Optional media area
- Eyebrow or label
- Title
- Supporting body
- Optional action row

## Typography
- Title: Omnes Narrow or Chennai depending on density
- Body: Transat
- Meta labels: Le Havre Rounded or Transat

## Spacing Rules
- Keep clear visual hierarchy between title and body.
- Preserve breathing room around media and action areas.
- Do not overfill cards with multiple display fonts.

## Accessibility Baseline
- Interactive cards must have clear focus behavior.
- Text overlays on media must maintain AA contrast.
