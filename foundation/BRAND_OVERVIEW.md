# Brand Overview

## Brand Name
RAINBOWSMOKE (d.b.a Mr. RainbowSmoke LLC)

## Positioning
A spectrum-forward creator and community brand built around live entertainment, creative expression, and authentic connection.

## Core Identity Signals
- Identity in motion
- Spectrum-forward color
- Blue-forward Pride emphasis
- High contrast and dark-friendly presentation
- Digital-native aesthetic

## Values
- Authenticity
- Inclusivity
- Creativity
- Community
- Excellence

## Audience
- Primary: creators, stream viewers, and community participants
- Secondary: collaborators, sponsors, media, and external partners

## Brand Promise
Create a welcoming, high-energy space where people can be fully expressive, visible, and connected.
